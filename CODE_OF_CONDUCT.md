# Code of Conduct

Yuheng follows the [Contributor Covenant, version 2.1](https://www.contributor-covenant.org/version/2/1/code_of_conduct/)
as its code of conduct. In short: be respectful, assume good faith, keep
discussion about the work, and do not harass anyone. Harassment, personal
attacks and discriminatory language are not acceptable in issues, pull requests,
discussions or any other project space.

## Enforcement

The maintainer may edit, hide or remove comments, close or lock threads, and
block people who break these rules. To report a problem, use GitHub's private
reporting (the **Security** tab → **Report a vulnerability**, see
[`SECURITY.md`](./SECURITY.md)) and say that it concerns conduct; reports are
handled privately, and the reporter's identity is kept confidential.
