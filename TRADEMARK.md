- Say truthfully that your work is based on, compatible with, or a fork of Yuheng

- Package and redeploy unmodified releases for your own use or your organization's.

## What you may not do without permission

- Distribute a modified version, or offer it as a hosted service, under the name

  think it is the official project.

  maintainer when it is not.

A fork that changes the code should carry its own name and logo. Forks are welcome;
this is only about not confusing users about which software they are running.

For a permission request or a question about a specific use, open an issue.
